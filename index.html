A Story Brewed
with Love

Lune began as a dream sketched on a napkin in 2018 — a café that didn't rush you out, where the coffee was always excellent, and where strangers left as friends.

We opened our doors in Lazimpat on a rainy March morning in 2019 with one espresso machine, two baristas, and an unwavering belief that great coffee deserves great company. Five years later, we've grown — but we've never forgotten why we started.

👩‍🍳
Asha Thapa
Co-founder & Head Barista
Trained in Melbourne and Tokyo, Asha brought world-class espresso technique home to Nepal.
👨‍💼
Roshan Shrestha
Co-founder & Chef
Former pastry chef from Paris, Roshan obsesses over the perfect croissant and builds every menu from scratch.

What We Stand For

Our Values

🫘
Radical Transparency

We share the name of every farm, the harvest date, and the price we paid. Coffee should come with a story.

🌍
Planet First

Compostable packaging, electric delivery bikes, rooftop herb garden. We're serious about leaving things better than we found them.

🤝
Community Roots

We host weekly events, support local artists, and host monthly "Pay It Forward" coffee days for those who need a warm cup.

Our Journey

Five Years in Motion

19
Doors Open
March 2019. One machine, two baristas, and a dream. We poured our first espresso on a rainy morning in Lazimpat.
20
First Award
Won "Best New Café" at the Kathmandu Food & Beverage Awards, just nine months after opening.
22
The Terrace
We expanded outdoors — 30-seat sun terrace with a living herb wall and string lights canopy.
24
Now & Beyond
Our in-house roastery launched this year. Every bean we serve is now roasted in Kathmandu, weekly.
